Estomato 0.3.0
==============

What's new
----------

- Currency totals: amounts prefixed with a symbol or label such as
  $, USD, BDT, RM, Yen or Won are now summed per currency, e.g.

      licence USD 40 and $ 10

  adds "Amount required [USD]: 40.00" and "Amount required [$]: 10.00"
  to the summary.

- Prefixed operators are now applied to hour, day and currency values:

      -1h rework credit     subtracts 1 hour
      *2 3h double shift    counts 6 hours
      +$5 bonus             adds 5

  Previously "-1h" was counted as +1 hour and "*2" was ignored, so totals
  from 0.2.0 may differ from what you see now.

Usage is unchanged: select text and run "Estomato: Run" from the
Command Palette.
